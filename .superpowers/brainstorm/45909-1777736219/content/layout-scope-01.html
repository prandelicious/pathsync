B. Live Cockpit, Summary Report

Keep live as a dashboard, but keep final output as a traditional text report optimized for logs and scrollback.

My recommendation: A

  • The user sees the same information hierarchy while copying and after completion.
  • Target verification can become a first-class block instead of being buried below counts.
  • It matches your earlier instinct: wide left work area, narrow right metrics box.

Tradeoff

  • The summary becomes more designed and less like a plain Unix report.
  • We need a graceful narrow-terminal fallback so logs remain readable.
