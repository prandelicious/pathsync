B. Keep Everything, Compress Hard

Retain all layout regions even when narrow, accepting aggressive truncation.

C. Require Wide Terminal

Do not attempt a rich layout below the wide threshold.

My recommendation: A

  • It avoids the exact problem you reported earlier: filenames and target names becoming unrecognizable.
  • The fallback keeps the most useful live signal: worker phase, item, size, target.
  • The wide layout can remain polished without forcing it into 80 columns.

Tradeoff

  • The narrow layout will show less information than the wide layout, especially per-target progress.
