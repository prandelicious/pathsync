C. More Decorative Unicode

Richer visual language, but more likely to look inconsistent across fonts and terminals.

My recommendation: A

  • It keeps the polished look you want in Ghostty/macOS while protecting logs and weaker terminals.
  • The current design already uses Unicode; we can make it intentional rather than accidental.
  • ASCII fallback can use the same layout model, just different glyphs.

Tradeoff

  • It adds a small formatting abstraction for glyph sets.
